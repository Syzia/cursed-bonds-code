the main gate of a secluded temple school hidden in forested mountains, seen
from the forest approach at standing eye height: a broad straight path of pale
flagstones leads away from the viewer through a grove of tall straight cedar
trees, lined on both sides by rows of mossy stone lanterns, to a large grey
stone torii gate standing across the path in the middle distance, and beyond
it wide stone steps rising to the first wooden temple hall with a heavy grey
tiled roof, softly veiled in mist, dappled sunlight and long shafts of light
falling through the cedars onto the path, ferns and moss along the edges,
early summer morning
