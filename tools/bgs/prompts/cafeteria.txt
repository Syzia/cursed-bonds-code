a spacious school dining hall inside a traditional Japanese wooden building
that is well looked after, seen from near the entrance: an open aisle of clean
polished floor in the foreground, then long rows of plain wooden dining tables
with chairs, a long serving counter with a stainless steel serving hatch and
neat stacks of trays along the back wall, tall windows with pale curtains tied
back down the left side looking out onto dense green forest, dark wooden beams
and pillars, pale plaster walls, round paper pendant lamps hanging from a high
ceiling, a water dispenser and a few potted plants by the wall, bright midday
sunlight falling across the tables
