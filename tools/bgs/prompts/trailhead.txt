the start of a mountain path at the top edge of a secluded temple school
hidden in forested mountains, seen at standing eye height: the last pale
flagstones of the campus path give way to packed earth under a small weathered
wooden gate without doors, and just beyond it the path splits three ways
around a mossy stone marker, the left branch running level into an ancient
cedar forest, the right branch dropping between ferns toward the glint of a
clear river below, and the middle branch climbing steeply up stone steps
toward a bare grassy ridge high above the trees, huge straight cedar trunks
on both sides, ferns and moss along the edges, soft shafts of sunlight through
the canopy, the near ground left open and empty, nothing built beyond the
gate, early summer morning
