# Antagonists

> **Revision 1.** The through-line is now a title, not a man. "The Strongest in
> History" pairs against Gojo's "the strongest of today" — two holders of one
> crown, one alive and one sealed in a casket on the far side of the campus.
> Wakuri below is no longer the top of the pyramid; he serves the resurrection.

One overarching villain across all three arcs, with an arc-level antagonist in
front of him each time. He is not a monster; he is an administrator, which is
the point.

---

## The instrument — Sadao Wakuri, "the Cartographer"

**Special grade curse user. Grading Office, Auxiliary Assessment Division.
Forty-one years of service. Immaculate record.**

Wakuri decides what grade a curse is. He has been deciding it wrongly, on
purpose, for a very long time.

His thesis is simple and monstrous: sorcerers who nearly die young become
stronger than sorcerers who are trained safely. So he maps where curses will
form — he is genuinely brilliant at it — and then files them one grade low,
near students he has chosen. The ones who survive are forged. The ones who
don't were going to break later anyway, and better now than in front of
someone who mattered.

He is not sadistic and he is never angry. He believes he is doing the
arithmetic nobody else is willing to do.

**Seeded in the current build:**
- ch2, on the tour: *"Somebody filed that street as clear this year. I've asked
  to see who."* — a special-grade-adjacent curse was in an alley behind an
  arcade, which is not where those live.
- ch4/ch5: the Sun Mall registered grade 3, was grade 2. Yaga:
  *"I have his name, and he will be hearing from me."*

That name is where Arc 1 goes.

**What he wants with the player.** The player awakened at eighteen with no
family line and no training, in the alley behind the arcade, a place Wakuri
chose. He has a file on them that is four years old. The awakening was not an
accident of fate; it was scheduled. Revealing *how* is the Arc 3 turn.

**How he appears.** Never in person until Arc 3. In Arc 1 he is a signature on
a form. In Arc 2 he is a voice on a phone. He is old, softly spoken, and
apologises more than Ijichi does — the difference is that he means it and does
it anyway.

## Arc 1 — Ryōsuke Nade, "the Surveyor"

**Grade 2 sorcerer. The man who filed the Sun Mall as a grade 3.**

Not a mastermind. A coward with a mortgage who has been signing what Wakuri
puts in front of him for six years, and has stopped letting himself notice.
He does a forty-minute survey on a four-storey building because a longer one
would find things he would then have to report.

Beatable by first-years. That is the point of him: the arc's antagonist is not
strong, he is *complicit*, and the horror is how ordinary that is.

**Arc: named at the debrief → his other filings found → cornered.** He does not
die. He gives up a map with the player's home town circled on it, in
handwriting that is not his, and then Wakuri stops returning his calls.

## Arc 2 — Kōji Mihara, "the Tally"

**Grade 1. Wakuri's assessor, embedded at the Kyoto Goodwill Event.**

He is there to count. The exchange event puts every promising student in one
place under supervised combat conditions, which is the cleanest assessment
opportunity of the year, and he has been attending for eleven years.

Where Nade signs things, Mihara *watches* — and during the event something is
released into it that was not on the schedule, to see who does what.

## Arc 3 — Wakuri, in person

Plus the thing he has been growing on the strength of everyone the first two
arcs failed to save.

## Rules for writing these

- Wakuri is never theatrical. He does paperwork. The most frightening line he
  has is an apology.
- No villain monologues an explanation the player hasn't already half-assembled.
- Curses stay inhuman and barely verbal. The *people* are the antagonists.
- Nade should be pitiable by the end of Arc 1 without being forgiven.
