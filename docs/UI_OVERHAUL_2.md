## 3b. The look test and the owner's ruling (6 Oct)

The owner ran the look test: the Courtyard, the Common Room and the Library,
each redone three ways from the current picture (A a clean anime frame, B a
real place, C a clean-up only). Measured afterwards: nothing moved in any of
the nine, so redoing a picture in place keeps its icons, exits and standing
spots. C changed almost nothing. The owner, verbatim:

> We should go with B, but we need to make it feel clean as-well. If you take
> a look at tge dorm they're leaves on the floor inside, Same goes for the
> library.
>
> The walls are also sure dirty, we still want to the clean anime look.
>
> I think we should just redo the whole library. Our current one feels to
> outdated.
>
> I also think we should redo the courtyard, All 3 buildings look the same.
> But all 3 lead to different locations. We want the map to flow. But also
> feel accurate.

Read as:
- **The look is settled: B, kept clean.** A real place (believable materials,
  light and depth, clear views through the windows) with a clean anime
  finish: fresh even walls, tidy floors with nothing scattered on them, crisp
  edges, clean colour, no grain. Nothing is added on seats, floors or open
  ground.
- **Not every picture is redone in place.** A room whose design is fine is
  redone from the current picture. A picture whose design is the problem is
  designed again: the **Library** from scratch, and the **Courtyard** so that
  its three buildings look different and each reads as what it leads to (the
  School Building, the way to the Dormitories, the Training Grounds).
- **"We want the map to flow. But also feel accurate."** The campus is one
  coherent place: what I see standing somewhere agrees with the map, an exit
  sits on the thing it leads to, each building has its own look and keeps it
  on the map, from the yards and at its door, and it stays true to the school
  as the series shows it. So the plan starts from one layout of the whole
  campus, with every building named, placed and described, and the map, the
  area maps and the outdoor pictures are made from that.

A second small test (`look_test_2`, five pictures) shows the settled look on
the Common Room and two readings each of the new Courtyard and the new
Library.

## 3d. The cut-out tests (7 Oct)

How a character is cut out of its render decides how clean every sprite's
outline is. Two rounds, both free: everything ran on this Mac.

**What was tried.** Five models from the ComfyUI-RMBG pack (RMBG-2.0, and
from its second family BiRefNet_toonout, which is trained on anime
characters, BiRefNet-general, BiRefNet-HR and BiRefNet-HR-matting); today's
own tool, `tools/keysprite.py`; and a prototype finishing step that reads the
edge off the flat backdrop colour.

**The pictures.** Maki's real render on pink. Today's sprites of Maki, Yuji,
Shoko and Nobara set on flat grey, where the true outline is known to the
pixel. The same four set on flat pink and flat green with crisp and with soft
edges, as a fresh render would be: sixteen pictures whose true cut-out is
known.

**Results.**
- **Finding the figure** (grey, four characters): the anime-trained model is
  the most reliable. At the two larger working sizes it lost 20 to 37 pixels
  of figure in all, where RMBG-2.0 lost 602 to 870 and the other three 66 to
  940. Its error along the outline is about a fifth lower than the next best.
- **No model's result is a finished cut-out on a flat backdrop.** Each leaves
  the backdrop's colour on the outline. On the exact pictures with crisp
  edges a model's result as it comes has 20,000 to 27,000 visibly wrong
  pixels (four characters together), against 15,600 for today's tool. The
  pack's "refine foreground" switch only darkens the edge colours: the pink
  stays. **The first round's advice, RMBG-2.0 with that switch at double
  working size, was wrong and is withdrawn.**
- **Today's tool** is clean: no backdrop colour is left. Its edges are hard.
- **The prototype** has 560 to 620 visibly wrong pixels on crisp edges, about
  twenty-five times fewer than today's tool, and 6,400 to 7,600 on soft edges
  against 35,700, about five times fewer. On Maki's real render it is level
  with today's tool by eye, with softer edges and fuller thin strands of
  hair, and it leaves a few dozen stray edge pixels where today's tool leaves
  a handful. It is not in `tools/` yet.
- **The second family cannot be used through the pack's own node on this
  Mac.** The node runs it in half precision on the processor: one picture ran
  for sixteen minutes. The same models run directly take 6 to 20 seconds.

**What follows.**
- New sprites are still rendered on a flat key colour that the character does
  not wear (pink for Maki, not pink for Yuji).
- The cut-out stays our own tool. The prototype's edge step is built into it
  in the art tools package and replaces today's keying only if it is at least
  as clean on every render we already have. The sixteen exact pictures become
  its tests.
- The anime-trained model is run directly beside it as a check (a gap left
  filled, a piece cut off, a stray mark), and it is the cutter for any picture
  that is not on a flat backdrop.
- The pack's nodes are not part of the sprite pipeline. rgthree's picture
  comparer goes into the look-test workflow files.

`~/Desktop/JJK_worktrees/ui2_design_work/cutout_test/`; the close-up sheets
`cutout_test_maki.jpg`, `cutout_test_maki_hand.jpg` (first round) and

## 4. How the review was read

- **Every campus location is redone.** The audit had counted the 24 campus
  pictures as finished (painted, one style). The owner's eye says otherwise,
  and that decides it.
- **"Actual sceneries", not "artworks":** settled by the look test, section
  3b: a real place, kept clean.
- **Redone in place where the design is fine:** such a picture is redone from
  the current one, same camera and layout, so the icons, exits and standing
  spots that sit on it stay where they are. Where the design is the problem
  (section 3b), the picture is composed again and everything on it is laid
  out again.
- **"Blend the characters into the background":** read as people who look
  like they stand in the room: a shadow under them, their colours matched to
  the room's light, a believable size and floor line, furniture in front of
  them where it should be; and, after the rooms are redone, painted poses of
  people doing things.
- **New places:** the four the owner named, more dormitories (asked on 5 Oct),
  and any others the plan proposes (the owner: "You can add some locations if
  you feel theyre needed").
- **The campus map is redone** with the new places on it.
