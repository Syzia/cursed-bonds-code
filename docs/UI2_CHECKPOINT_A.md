# Checkpoint A: what to play, and two questions

*Jujutsu Kaisen: Cursed Bonds · the second UI overhaul · wave 0 finished on
12 Oct 2026. About 30 minutes, or 15 without the optional new game. This is
the owner's page for `docs/UI2_DESIGN.md` 10.5, row A.*

## What this checkpoint is

Wave 0 changed **how the campus is laid out and who is where**. It did not
change how anything looks. No new picture is in the game yet: the eleven new
places show borrowed pictures, and the pictures you chose go in after this
checkpoint, starting with the Courtyard, the Library, the Common Room and the
Cafeteria. So judge how it *plays*, not how it looks. **There is nothing to
approve.**

## Three buttons that make it quick

Run the game from the Ren'Py launcher as usual. In any place, the small
**Review tools** button at the bottom left opens a panel. Three of its buttons
are all you need here:

| button | what it does | why you want it |
|---|---|---|
| **+20 bond with everyone** | adds 20 Bond with everyone you know | a friend's bedroom door asks for Bond 15; press this once and the doors open |
| **Jump to Night** | it is Night, today | most of what is new happens at Night |
| **Jump to day +7** | a week later, on the same weekday | to reach a later week without sleeping through it |

The day and its weekday are at the top left of the screen. Sleeping moves to
the next day.

## Play this

1. **Load one of your old saves.** It opens where you left it. (One of your
   eleven, the autosave `auto-10` in the prologue, opens a few lines further
   on in the same scene; the other ten open exactly where they were.)
2. **Walk to your room.** Dormitory Garden, then the Boys' Dormitory, then My
   Room at the end of the corridor. Sleep, and wake.
3. **Walk into each new place once.** They are on borrowed pictures for now.
   - Off the **Dormitory Garden**: the Boys' Dormitory, with Yuji's room,
     Megumi's room and the room Panda and Toge share; and the Girls'
     Dormitory, with Nobara's room and Maki's.
   - Off the **Staff Building Steps**: the Teachers' Lounge, and the
     Teachers' Dormitory above it.
   - Off the **Mountain Path**: the Onsen, and its Sauna.
   - Off the **Training Yard**: the Swimming Pool.
4. **A weekday Night** (Monday to Friday; press *Jump to Night*). Visit Yuji,
   who is up. Visit Megumi, who is asleep. Find Yaga at his workbench in the
   Teachers' Dormitory.
5. **A Sunday Night.** Look into the Library: Megumi is there, studying late.
   (On a Wednesday Night it is Toge.) The Library still looks as it does
   today; its lamps come on once its new pictures are installed.
6. *Optional, twenty minutes more:* start a new game and play to the
   Courtyard.

## The two questions

- **Is one more click to reach your door on foot fine?** Your room used to
  open straight off the garden; it is now down a corridor.
- **Does who is awake on which Nights feel right?**

| at Night | awake | asleep |
|---|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | Yuji, Nobara, Toge | Megumi, Maki, Panda |
| Saturday and Sunday | Megumi, Maki, Panda | Yuji, Nobara, Toge |
| every night | Yaga at his bench, Shoko in the infirmary | Utahime, Ijichi |

Two of them are out late once a week, in the Library and awake: **Megumi on
Sundays, Toge on Wednesdays.** Their own rooms are empty on those Nights.

## If something looks wrong

Say it in your own words, with a screenshot if that is easy. Anything you
change here becomes a rule for what is built next.

## What happens after you have played

The first four pictures go into the game (the Courtyard, the Library, the
Common Room and the Cafeteria), and the Courtyard and the Library become the
first two places where you click the picture itself. Then the other places
follow, one sitting at a time, and six parts of the game are built side by
side (who is where and what they say, the map and its doors, people standing
in the rooms, things to do, the baths and the pool, the menus).
