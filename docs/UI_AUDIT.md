# The UI audit: what the game still lacks on the presentation side

*6 Oct 2026. Read-only audit of the game at commit 5f9da45 (phase 2 merged): seven auditors, one per subject, each checked by a second reader; then a merge, a completeness check and a follow-up. 134 items. The full data, with each auditor's inventory tables, is `docs/ui_audit_data.json`.*

> **Read this first.** The audit judged from the files, and from the files the 24 campus pictures look finished. The owner's review of the same day says otherwise: every location is to be redone, because they do not feel alive (grain, vague views through the windows). The owner's word is in `docs/UI_OVERHAUL_2.md` and wins wherever the two differ. Where this audit says a campus picture is "painted" or "in good shape", read: it exists, and it is on the list to be redone. Some questions below are already answered there (sprites for the block figures: yes; dormitory rooms: rooms to walk into; my room: inside the boys' dormitory).

## Overview

This is the merged list from seven audits of the game at commit 5f9da45, each checked by a second reader, plus a follow-up check of eight gaps a critic found (all eight were real; one turned out wider than stated). Nothing was run or changed. The working folder is on branch ui2, at the same commit as main. In the evidence, "game/" means /Users/hunterjolly/Desktop/JJKgame.md/game, "docs/" and "tools/" sit beside it, and "shots/" means /private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-hunterjolly-Desktop-JJKgame-md/e3d2f368-fc62-40d2-bb70-0a23434a2d40/scratchpad.

The short version:

- The campus is in good shape. All 24 places, the six area maps and the Tokyo map are painted, and all 11 campus people have a full sprite with six faces. One caution from the follow-up: the backgrounds come in two looks, a soft dim one (the three offices, the Library) and a bright crisp one (courtyard, gate, ridge), judged from 8 of the 51 pictures.
- None of the places you asked for exists: no boys' dormitory, no teachers' lounge or quarters. At Night five people are simply "off campus" (Megumi, Toge, Yaga, Utahime, Ijichi), Yuji and Panda stand in the garden, and Nobara and Maki are behind a door that never opens, because nothing in the game ever gives the invitation.
- Everything off campus is a stand-in. Ten flat drawn pictures and eight tinted copies of them carry the whole prologue, the Sun Mall, the Nade missions, all eight board jobs and all five Tokyo districts. The first level of the campus map is also a flat drawing. Your own stand-in list in docs/PLAYTEST.md leaves most of these out.
- People stand; they do not do things, and never with each other. Of 43 person-and-place pairs in the weekly routine, 2 have a painted pose, 12 more are planned, and 21 do not even have their own caption. Four of the twelve planned poses are written for an object the picture does not show (a desk, a vending machine, the veil, a car). A painted pose disappears the moment you click the person.
- After the prologue, faces stop moving. The 25 character scenes have no face change in about 825 lines, although every person has six faces painted. This is a script pass, not art.
- 13 of the 24 places have nothing in the picture to click except exits, and the pictures are full of painted things that do nothing (the pond, the striking posts, Gojo's TV, the board-game boxes).
- Nothing teaches a new player how the place view works, and icon names show only under a mouse pointer with no setting to change that.
- The menus, save screen, choice lists and quick menu are still Ren'Py's stock screens in the game's colours. There is no sound at all.
- The card-table invite, chess, memory and tic-tac-toe are asked for and not built.
- The storyboard's new cast (18 people and creatures, Sukuna in Yuji's body first) has no sprites; that belongs mostly to the story acts but is the largest single art cost on the list.

Art already planned and priced is small (twelve poses and blink-and-talk for ten people, about $6.72). The rest of the list is new; see the art estimate. No prompt file exists for any missing background, so each needs one written before you can run anything.
