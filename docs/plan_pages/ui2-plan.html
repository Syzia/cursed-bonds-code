A rule the plan sets

Nobody is ever drawn or described in the bath, the sauna, the cold plunge or the pool's water, and nothing romantic is offered at the onsen, the sauna or the pool. People there stand in the rest hall or on the deck as their usual sprites, in uniform. A soak or a swim is a few lines about heat, cold and tiredness over the picture. The tools refuse to make a painted pose in any of the three.

What you do

Checkpoints

Four, played from the Ren'Py launcher as before.

Art sittings

Twenty-eight, of twelve pictures or fewer. Most are: drag one file into ComfyUI, press Run, say which version you like. Each comes with a page of numbered steps.

Two-file sittings

Eleven sittings have two files. You run the first and say which results you accept. They are installed, which takes about ten minutes. Then you run the second, which is made from the first: a room's evening from its new day picture, for example.

The four new people

Dad, Mom, Kurose and Nade are a different kind of sitting: a base picture made on Comfy Cloud, then two or three short repair runs in your own ComfyUI. Allow an evening for Dad and less for the others.

What the tools do

They check that nothing moved, vanished or was added, and say in plain words if a picture has to be run again. For a room redone in place the result is in the game as soon as you relaunch. For a yard, a map or a new place, its layout is done for you after you choose a version, which takes about forty minutes.

Credits are not the limit

You have said not to worry about credits and to go for the best possible quality. So nothing is cut for cost, and the list of things that would have waited is gone.

The plan made a few compromises to save money. It is being revised to take them back, and the total will rise from about $36. You will get the new figure with the revised plan. The compromises were:

  • Evenings and nights were painted only where people stand after dark. Everywhere else kept a tint, which leaves daylight in the windows.
  • Simple rooms got one version to accept, not two to choose from.
  • The teachers at home stayed as standing sprites, with no painted poses.

How the plan was checked

The plan was built from three competing designs, scored by three judges and merged into one. It was then reviewed twice by independent readers working against the game's code and your pictures.

First review

45 problems, none blocking. All corrected.

Second review

59 problems, one blocking: the lists did not agree that nobody is ever painted at the baths or the pool. That is now a firm rule. All corrected.

Confirming read

Ten left, all about how the builders prove their work. All ten were corrected and checked again. Four small edits made after that have not had a second reader.

What held up

Every count and price on this page against the detailed tables. The campus layout, by hand: what stands left, right and ahead in each yard, and where each map pin goes. That old saves keep their place, including saves made inside story scenes.

Only trying can tell

Whether a yard composed from words and its neighbours' pictures agrees with them. Your Courtyard was made this way and worked; the first yard is the next test. Whether a painted person matches the standing sprites in the new look. Three are made before the other nineteen.
