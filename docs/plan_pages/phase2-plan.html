The UI overhaul, drawn from your playtest notes and your answers. Nothing here is built yet. The pictures below are sketches made with your real backgrounds and sprites, so you can judge the layout before any credits are spent on building it.

見At a glance

What changes

A calmer map in two levels. Icons in place of words. A journal built around each person. Grade colours everywhere. A night rule with two exceptions. Rooms you can poke at: a computer, a card table, fishing, a Library. People who look like they belong in the room.

What doesn't

Your saves. Every screen keeps its name and what it hands back to the game, so no save needs converting.

How it's built

Wiring first, then five pieces at once, then three, then a final pass. You play four checkpoints along the way.

Art

About 47 pictures, about $12.50 in all, in five small batches. Everything has a stand-in, so the build never waits for a picture.

From you

Four decisions still open, each with a default that gets built if you say nothing. They're at the end. You approved the plan on 1 Oct; the build has started.

決Your calls

Five decisions. The first four each have a default that gets built unless you say otherwise, and you can answer now or at checkpoint A. The fifth you've decided.

  1. How strict is the night? DefaultAt Night nothing that takes time is allowed, apart from the exceptions. Wait becomes "Go to bed". OrNight stays usable, and Kaito just wakes up in his room afterwards.
  2. When do the character scenes start? DefaultAfter roll call, on day 2. They still narrate in "you" until the story phase converts them. OrAfter the Sun Mall, around day 4. That keeps "you" further from the prologue but leaves the first days with no bond scenes.
  3. Two-hour activities and a waiting story scene DefaultA trip into town with someone, a day off together or the Tokyo trip ends an hour early when a story scene is waiting, so the scene isn't pushed to tomorrow. The hour isn't lost. A spar is one hour and is never cut short. OrLeave it as it is: the scene comes a day late.
  4. The look of the icons and grade chips DefaultJudged from a sample screen in the game at checkpoint A, since a sketch on this page can't stand in for the real thing.
  5. The Gojo night and your grade's ceiling DecidedThe night may push past the ceiling: Control up to 6 above it, and the reserve up to 3 above its own. So it's offered at every grade whenever it's due. If Control already sits 6 above the ceiling from an earlier night, the next one waits for a promotion.

Chosen for you

Say the word and any of these changes. Each is one number in a table.

  • Cards: stakes of ¥100, ¥500 or ¥1,000. A sitting is five hands at most. At most ¥6,000 won in a day. The table is closed at Night.
  • The Gojo night: whatever the best day of training would give (his lesson, four sessions on the ridge and Hold the hour), plus 6 Control and 3 reserve. From Control 30 that's +29 Control and +12 reserve, against a best day's +23 and +9. At most once every ten days.
  • The day after: training, Gojo's lesson, Hold the hour and swimming are closed. Studying, eating, talking, cards, fishing and the computer stay open, but nothing raises Control or Output that day.
  • A night in Tokyo: ¥1,500 for the club. You wake with half stamina and 70% cursed energy. Not two nights running.
  • The map moves you on the second click. Clicking an area only opens it.
  • Small numbers: studying in the Library gives Insight +4, against +3 at a desk. The vending machine is ¥150 for 10 stamina, once a day.
